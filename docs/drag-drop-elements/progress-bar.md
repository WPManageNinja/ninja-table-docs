# Progress Element

The **Progress** element shows a percentage as a bar, a circle or a gauge in a Drag & Drop table cell, for example skill levels in a team table or how full a course is. It is in the **Advanced** group and is free.

To add one, click a cell and click **Progress** in the **Elements** tab. Click it to set its options in the **Settings** tab.

![Progress element](/images/drag-drop-elements/progress-bar/1.-Progress-bar-element.webp)

## Progress options

- **Progress type**: **line**, **circle** (default) or **dashboard** (a gauge).
- **Progressbar percentage**: the value to show.
- **Progress bar color**: the color of the filled part.
- **Text color** and **Text font size**: the percentage label.
- **Progressbar width**: the size of the bar or circle.
- **Progressbar thickness**: how thick the bar or ring is.
- **Alignment**: left, center or right.
- **Spacing**: margin and padding around it. See [Element Spacing](/spacing).

Click **Save** to keep your changes.
