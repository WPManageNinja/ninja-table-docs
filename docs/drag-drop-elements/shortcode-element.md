# Shortcode Element

The **Shortcode** element shows the output of any WordPress shortcode inside a Drag & Drop table cell. Use it to place a form, a booking button, a video player or content from another plugin inside your table.

## Add a shortcode

1. Copy the shortcode from the other plugin, for example a Fluent Forms form shortcode.
2. In the builder, click a cell and click **Shortcode** in the **Elements** tab.
3. Click the element and paste the shortcode into the box in the **Settings** tab.
4. Set the **Alignment** (left, center or right) and, if needed, the [spacing](/spacing).
5. Click **Save**.

![Paste a form shortcode](/images/drag-drop-elements/shortcode-element/1.-Fluent-Forms-shortcode.webp)

The builder shows the shortcode text; the real output appears on your site and in **Preview**.
