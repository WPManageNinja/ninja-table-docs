# Ribbon Element (Pro)

The **Ribbon** element (Pro) adds a label such as "Most popular" or "Best value" across the corner or edge of a cell, so one plan or product stands out.

To add one, click a cell and click **Ribbon** in the **Advanced** group of the **Elements** tab. Click it to set its options in the **Settings** tab, which has three sub-tabs.

![Ribbon element in a table](/images/drag-drop-elements/ribbon-element/1.-Ribbon-element-in-table.webp)

## General

- **Ribbon type**: **side** (default), **bookmark**, **corner** or **horizontal**.
- **Ribbon position**: left or right.
- **Ribbon width** and **Ribbon height**.
- **X offset** and **Y offset**: move the ribbon from its default spot.
- **Table margin top**: add space above the table so a ribbon on the top row isn't cut off.

![Ribbon general options](/images/drag-drop-elements/ribbon-element/2.-Ribbon-element.webp)

## Text

- **Ribbon text**: the label, for example "Most popular".
- **Font color**, **Font size** and **Font style**.
- **Font X offset** and **Font Y offset**: position the text on the ribbon.

![Ribbon text options](/images/drag-drop-elements/ribbon-element/3.-Ribbon-text.webp)

## Background

- **Background color**: the ribbon's color.

![Ribbon background color](/images/drag-drop-elements/ribbon-element/4.-Ribbon-background-color.webp)

The Ribbon has no Spacing section; use the offsets instead. Click **Save** to keep your changes.
