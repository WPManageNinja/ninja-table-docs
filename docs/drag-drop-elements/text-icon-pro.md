# Text Icon Element (Pro)

The **Text Icon** element (Pro) shows a short piece of text with an icon beside it, such as a phone icon next to a number or a clock next to opening hours. In the Settings tab it is called "Text icon".

To add one, click a cell and click **Text Icon** in the **Advanced** group of the **Elements** tab. Click it to set its options in the **Settings** tab, which has three sub-tabs.

![Text Icon element](/images/drag-drop-elements/text-icon/1.-Text-Icon-Drag-and-Drop.webp)

## General

- **Text**: the text to show.
- **Item spacing**: space between the icon and the text.
- **Alignment**: left, center or right.

![Text Icon general options](/images/drag-drop-elements/text-icon/2.-Text-Icon-element-2.webp)

## Text

- **Font color**, **Font size** and **Font style**.

![Text Icon text options](/images/drag-drop-elements/text-icon/3.-text-icon-customize.webp)

## Icon

- **Color** and **Icon size**.
- Pick an icon, using the search box, or **Upload your own**.
- **Icon position**: left or right of the text.

![Text Icon icon options](/images/drag-drop-elements/text-icon/4.-text-icon-color.webp)

Below the sub-tabs, **Spacing** sets the margin and padding around the element; see [Element Spacing](/spacing). Click **Save** to keep your changes.
