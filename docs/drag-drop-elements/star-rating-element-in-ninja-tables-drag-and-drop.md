# Rating Element

The **Rating** element shows a star rating in a Drag & Drop table cell, for example to rate products or plans. In the Settings tab it is called **Star rating**.

To add one, click a cell and click **Rating** in the **Elements** tab. Click the stars to set the rating and options in the **Settings** tab.

![Rating element in a table](/images/drag-drop-elements/star-rating-element-ninja/1.-Rating-element-in-tables.webp)

## Rating options

- **Star size**
- **Alignment**: left, center or right.
- **Color**: the color of the filled stars.
- **Show rating value**: show the number next to the stars (off by default).
- **Maximum star**: the total number of stars (3–10).
- **Spacing**: margin and padding around the rating. See [Element Spacing](/spacing).

Click **Save** to keep your changes.
