# Rating Element

The **Rating** element shows a star rating in a Drag & Drop table cell, for example to rate products or plans. It is free. In the Settings tab it is titled **Star Rating**.

To add one, click a cell and click **Rating** in the **Elements** tab. If no single cell is selected, it goes into the last (bottom-right) cell. The new rating starts at 5 stars. Click the element and use the **Rating** control at the top of the **Settings** tab to set the score. Half stars are allowed. You can also click the stars on the canvas.

![Rating element with 5 stars and the Rating control in the Settings tab](/images/drag-drop-elements/star-rating-element-ninja/1.-Rating-Element-Showing-4-5-Out.webp)

## Rating options

Under **Content & style**:

- **Rating**: click a star to set the score, for example 4.5.
- **Maximum Star** (3–10, default 5): the total number of stars.
- **Star Size** (10–50, default 20).
- **Color** (default `#F2C009`): the color of the filled stars.
- **Show Rating Value** (off by default): show the score next to the stars, for example "4.5/5".
- **Alignment**: **Left**, **Center** (default) or **Right**.

Under **Content & style**, the **Spacing** section sets the margin and padding around the rating; see [Element Spacing](/guide/drag-drop-settings/spacing).

Click **Save** to keep your changes.
