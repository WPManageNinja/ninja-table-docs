# How to Get Support

If the documentation doesn't answer your question, the WPManageNinja support team can help with the plugin and with your own tables. This page shows where to open a ticket and what to include in it.

## Open a support ticket

1. In Ninja Tables, click **Help** in the top bar.
2. On the **Need expert support** card, click **Open a ticket**. This opens the [WPManageNinja support portal](https://wpmanageninja.com/support-tickets/).
3. Log in to your WPManageNinja account, or create one, and start a new ticket.

The **What to include** button on the same card opens this page.

## What to include in your ticket

The more detail you give, the faster the team can help. Include:

- **What you're trying to do**, and what happens instead.
- **Steps to reproduce** the problem, in order.
- **Screenshots or a screen recording**.
- **Error messages**, copied exactly.
- **The table**: its ID or shortcode, and the page where it's shown.
- **Your WordPress version.**
- **Your Ninja Tables version**, and your Ninja Tables Pro version if you use Pro.

You'll find your plugin versions under **Plugins** › **Installed Plugins**, and your WordPress version under **Dashboard** › **Updates**.

::: tip
If a table on your site shows old data, try [clearing the caches](/clear-table-cache) first. If you use Pro and haven't activated your license yet, see [Activate your Pro license](/upgrade-to-ninja-tables-pro-add-on-and-activate-ninja-tables-pro-license-key).
:::
