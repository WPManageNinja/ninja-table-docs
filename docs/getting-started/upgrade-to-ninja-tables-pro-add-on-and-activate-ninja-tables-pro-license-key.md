# Upgrade and Activate Ninja Tables Pro License Key

**Ninja Tables Pro** is an add-on that unlocks the Pro features, such as connected data sources, custom filters and frontend editing. It runs on top of the free Ninja Tables plugin, so keep the free plugin installed and active. To see what Pro adds, read [Free vs Pro](/free-vs-pro).

## Buy and download Ninja Tables Pro

1. Go to the [Ninja Tables Pro page on wpmanageninja.com](https://wpmanageninja.com/downloads/ninja-tables-pro-add-on/) and buy a plan. You can also click **Get Pro** in the Ninja Tables top bar, or **Get Pro** under **Ninja Tables** in the WordPress admin menu. Both show only while Pro isn't active.
2. Sign up for a WPManageNinja account if you don't have one. Your account holds your license key, your downloads and your support tickets.
3. Complete the payment, then log in to your account dashboard and click **Download**. The add-on downloads as a .zip file.

From the same dashboard you can click **Upgrade** to move to a bigger plan later.

## Install the Pro add-on

1. In your WordPress admin, go to **Plugins** › **Add New** › **Upload Plugin**.
2. Choose the Ninja Tables Pro .zip file and click **Install Now**.
3. Click **Activate Plugin**.

The plan pill in the Ninja Tables top bar changes from **Free** to **Pro**, and the **Get Pro** button disappears.

::: warning
Don't deactivate or delete the free **Ninja Tables** plugin. Ninja Tables Pro doesn't work without it.
:::

## Activate your license key

Activating the license gives you automatic updates and support. Until you activate it, the WordPress admin menu under **Ninja Tables** shows an **Activate License** link (or **Renew License** if your license has expired).

1. Copy your license key from your WPManageNinja account dashboard.
2. In Ninja Tables, click the **gear** icon in the top bar to open **Settings**, then click **License**. The **Activate License** menu link opens the same screen.
3. Paste the key into **Enter the License Key**. One key covers the number of sites your plan allows.
4. Click **Activate**.

![Settings License screen with the license key field and Activate button](/images/getting-started/upgrade-ninja-tables/2.-Settings-License-Screen-With-The-License.webp)

Once the key is active, the License screen shows your plan, a status badge (for example **Active**), **Expires** (or **Lifetime**) with the days left, the masked **License key**, **Licensed to** and **Last checked**.

## Manage your license

On **Settings** › **License** you can:

- Click **Refresh** to check the license status again.
- Click **Deactivate** to free the key, for example before moving the license to another site.
- Click **Use a different key** to replace the key with a new one.
- Open **Your account** to manage your purchase on wpmanageninja.com, or **Get Support** to ask for help.

The status badge can read **Active**, **Expired**, **Disabled**, **Invalid**, **Not activated** or **Grace period**. A message under the badge explains what to do:

- **Grace period**: everything keeps working for a short time before updates and support end. Renew before it runs out.
- **Expired**: updates have stopped. Click **Renew license** to renew it.
- **Disabled**: this usually means a refund or a chargeback. Use **Get Support** to ask about it.
- **Could not check your license**: the license check failed. Click **Refresh** to try again.

::: tip
If the License screen says **Please update Ninja Tables Pro**, your Pro version is older than the free plugin. Update Ninja Tables Pro from **Plugins** › **Installed Plugins**, then open the screen again.
:::
