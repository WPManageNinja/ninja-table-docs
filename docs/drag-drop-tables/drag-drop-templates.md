# Drag & Drop Templates

**Templates** give you a finished Drag & Drop table design to start from, so you only change the text, prices and links. There are 15 templates in four categories; 6 are free and the other 9 need Ninja Tables Pro.

## Use a template

1. Go to **Ninja Tables** › **Create Table** › **Drag & Drop table**.
2. Enter a **Table name**, choose **Choose a template** and click **Next**.
3. Filter by category (**All**, **Pricing**, **Comparison**, **Employee** or **Schedule**), click a template and click **Create table**.

The table opens in the builder, where you can change everything. Click **Save** once before adding it to a page; see [Create a Drag & Drop Table](/how-to-create-your-first-drag-and-drop-table-with-ninja-tables).

## Available templates

| Category | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| **Pricing** | Pricing Table One | Pricing Table Two, Three, Four |
| **Comparison** | Comparison Table One | Comparison Table Two, Three, Four, Five |
| **Employee** | Employee Table One | Employee Table Two, Three |
| **Schedule** | Schedule Table One, Two, Three | None |

Comparison Table Four includes a highlighted column, set with **Highlight column** in [Cell Settings](/drag-drop-cell-settings).

## Pro templates

Pro templates carry a **PRO** badge. The badge shows on these cards even when Ninja Tables Pro is active, so it marks which templates are Pro, not which are locked.

On the free version you can still select a Pro template, but clicking **Create table** shows "This is a Pro feature." and nothing is created. Choose a free template or upgrade to continue.

::: tip
Some templates come with **Enable responsive table** turned off, so they keep their desktop layout on phones. To stack them on small screens, turn it on in the table settings; see [Mobile & Tablet Layout](/responsive-table-design).
:::
