# Drag & Drop Templates

**Templates** give you a finished Drag & Drop table design to start from, so you only change the text, prices and links. There are 15 templates in four categories; 6 are free and the rest need Ninja Tables Pro.

## Use a template

1. Go to **Ninja Tables** › **Create Table** › **Drag & Drop table**.
2. Enter a **Table name**, choose **Choose a template** and click **Next**.
3. Filter by category, click a template and click **Create table**.

The table opens in the builder, where you can change everything. Click **Save** once before adding it to a page; see [Create a Drag & Drop Table](/how-to-create-your-first-drag-and-drop-table-with-ninja-tables).

## Available templates

| Category | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| **Pricing** | Pricing Table One | Pricing Table Two, Three, Four |
| **Comparison** | Comparison Table One | Comparison Table Two, Three, Four, Five |
| **Employee** | Employee Table One | Employee Table Two, Three |
| **Schedule** | Schedule Table One, Two, Three | — |

On the free version, choosing a Pro template shows "This is a Pro feature." and opens the upgrade page.

::: tip
Some templates come with **Responsive** turned off, so they keep their desktop layout on phones. To stack them on small screens, turn on **Enable responsive table** in the table settings; see [Mobile & Tablet Layout](/responsive-table-design).
:::
