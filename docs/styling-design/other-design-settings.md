# Sticky Header & First Column

**Sticky header & column** (Pro) keeps the header row at the top of the screen while visitors scroll down a long table, and keeps the first column in place while they scroll sideways through a wide one.

1. Go to **Ninja Tables** › **Tables** and open a table.
2. Click the **Appearance** tab, then the **Features** tab in the settings panel.
3. Open **Sticky header & column**.

![Sticky header settings](/images/styling-design/other-design-settings/1.-New-Ui-table-design-Other-tab.webp)

## Settings

- **Sticky header**: keep the header row visible while scrolling down.
  - **Top offset**: move the sticky header down so it isn't hidden behind your theme's fixed header. Enter a positive or negative number, or a jQuery element.
  - **Turn off on mobile**: don't make the header sticky on phones.
- **Sticky first column**: keep the first column visible while scrolling sideways.

Click **Save** to keep your changes.
