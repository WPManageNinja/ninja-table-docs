# Sticky Header & First Column

**Sticky Header & Column** (Pro) keeps the header row at the top of the screen while visitors scroll down a long table, and keeps the first column in place while they scroll sideways through a wide one.

1. Go to **Ninja Tables** › **Tables** and open a table.
2. Click the **Appearance** tab, then the **Features** tab in the settings panel.
3. Open **Sticky Header & Column**. The section has a Pro badge, and the switches inside it are locked on the free version.

![Sticky Header & Column section with Sticky Header, Top Offset and Sticky First Column](/images/styling-design/other-design-settings/1.-Sticky-Header-Column-Section-With-Sticky.webp)

## Settings

- **Sticky Header**: keep the header row visible while scrolling down. It is off by default. Turning it on shows two more options:
  - **Top Offset**: move the sticky header down so it isn't hidden behind your theme's fixed header. Enter a positive or negative number, or a jQuery element. Empty means no offset.
  - **Turn Off On Mobile**: don't make the header sticky on phones.
- **Sticky First Column**: keep the first column visible while scrolling sideways. It is off by default.

Click **Save** to keep your changes.
