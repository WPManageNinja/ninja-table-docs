# Table Design Overview

::: doc-tree appearance hero

## What do you want to do?

Pick the goal that sounds like yours. Each card takes you straight to the right guide.

::: doc-tree appearance tasks

## Find any setting, tab by tab

This works like the panel in the plugin. Pick a tab, open a setting to see what it changes on your table and what visitors will notice, then jump to the full guide. Only one setting stays open at a time, just like in the panel.

::: doc-tree appearance panel

## Where to find it in the plugin

1. Go to **Ninja Tables** › **Tables** and open a table.
2. Click the **Table Design** tab.

The live preview is on the left and the settings panel is on the right.

![Table Design tab with the live preview on the left and the settings panel on the right](/images/styling-design/table-style-settings/1.-Appearance-Tab-With-The-Live-Preview.webp)

## The whole panel on one map

Prefer the big picture? Every tab, setting and option on one page. Click a setting to open its guide.

::: doc-tree appearance map

## How the panel works

The settings panel has three tabs: **Styling**, **Features** and **Colors**. It opens on **Styling**. In **Styling** and **Features**, sections are accordions: click a heading to open it, and only one section stays open at a time. A section with a switch in its heading (**Search**, **Sorting**, **Pagination**) turns that whole feature on or off. Stacking rows as cards is a switch inside **Mobile**.

## The live preview

The preview is drawn by your active theme, the way visitors will see it, including your theme's fonts and styles. Unsaved changes show in the preview until you save or leave the page.

- **Preview Width**: switch between **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile** to check each screen size.

Click **Save** at the top of the editor to keep your changes. Table Design settings and the Export & Print buttons are saved together.

::: tip
The preview needs at least one column. If the table has none yet, add them in [Table Rows](/working-in-table-rows) first.
:::

## Set the starting design for new tables

Table Design changes apply to the open table only. To choose the look every new table starts with, use the gear icon › **Settings** › **Global appearance**. See [Default Design for New Tables](/use-design-as-default).
